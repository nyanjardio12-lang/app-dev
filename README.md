# My Favorite Movie

## Spider-Man

**Spider-Man** is my favorite superhero movie because it has action, adventure, comedy, and an interesting story. It shows how Peter Parker uses his powers to help and protect people.

### Why I Like This Movie
- It has exciting action scenes.
- Spider-Man is brave and responsible.
- The story is interesting.
- It teaches the importance of helping others.
- It shows the value of friendship and family.

### My Favorite Character

**Peter Parker (Spider-Man)** – I like him because he is brave, kind, and always tries to help other people.

### Favorite Quote

> With great power comes great responsibility.

### Movie Rating

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5/5**

### My Opinion

For me, Spider-Man is a great movie because it teaches us to be responsible, help others, and make good decisions.
